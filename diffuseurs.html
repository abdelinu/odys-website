How to use it

Three sticks is the number.

More rattan is not more scent — it is a room you stop noticing and a bottle that empties in three weeks. Three sticks is what we ship because it is what lasts.

  1. 01

    Open and place

    On a stable surface, away from direct sun and radiators.

  2. 02

    Insert the three sticks

    Let them soak for an hour before the first turn.

  3. 03

    Turn them weekly

    Wet side up. That is the entire maintenance.

  4. 04

    Small room, fewer sticks

    In a bathroom, use two. In an open salon, three.
