The ritual

How to burn a stick properly.

A bâtonnet is not a candle. Most of the scent is lost in the first thirty seconds if you leave the flame on it.

  1. 01

    Light the tip

    Let the flame catch for three or four seconds, no longer.

  2. 02

    Blow it out

    You want an ember, not a fire. The smoke should be thin and steady.

  3. 03

    Stand it upright

    In sand, a holder or a mabkhara — away from curtains and draughts.

  4. 04

    Dress the fabric

    Hold a jacket or scarf over the smoke. Wool keeps oud for days.
